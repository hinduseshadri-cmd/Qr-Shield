About

AI-Based QR Code Attack Detection for Mobile Security

QR Shield

An intelligent security application that uses AI-powered rule-based detection to identify malicious QR codes before they can harm your device or compromise your data.

Objectives

  • Real-time QR code scanning via device camera
  • AI-based threat classification (Safe/Suspicious/Malicious)
  • URL structure analysis and risk assessment
  • Server-side scan history (Node.js backend)
  • Detailed reporting and dashboard analytics
